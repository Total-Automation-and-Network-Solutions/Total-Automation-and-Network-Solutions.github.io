Since 2011

We've spent 15 years with our hands inside control panels

No fancy origin story here. We started out wiring PLCs and pulling cable through factory ceilings, and we got good at it because we had to. Somewhere along the way, "good at automation" turned into "why isn't this data going anywhere?" That question is basically what's driving the company now.

Automation and networking are still core to our work. The platform is something new we're building in parallel, not a replacement.

What we do every day

Panels, cabling and getting it right on-site

  • Fire alarm, CCTV, public address & building automation systems
  • Panel wiring, PLC & relay-based control systems
  • Hands-on system integration and on-site support
  • 2023: reorganised as Total Automation & Network Solutions to match where we were headed

In development, not yet live

Our flagship build: a smart meter platform for multi-site operations

One dashboard for every site. Operators get live consumption data, remote relay control and prepaid recharge, with each client's data kept fully separate from the next. We're building this in-house and it isn't ready for a live demo yet, but we're taking early conversations with operators who want a say in how it works.

  • Real-time meter readings
  • Remote relay on/off control
  • Prepaid recharge & coupons
  • Role-based, multi-tenant access
  • Full audit trail on every action
  • Live data over MQTT
